FFmpeg (LGPL v3)
----------------
AirLens uses libavcodec, libavutil and libswresample from FFmpeg 8.1 to decode
video on the GPU. The files ffmpeg\avcodec-62.dll, ffmpeg\avutil-60.dll and
ffmpeg\swresample-6.dll are unmodified FFmpeg libraries.

  License: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 (LGPL v3). The full
           text is in ffmpeg\LICENSE.txt. The LGPL builds on the GNU General
           Public License, version 3: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.txt
  Build:   ffmpeg-n8.1-latest-win64-lgpl-shared-8.1 from
           https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds (LGPL configuration, no GPL
           or nonfree components)
  Source:  https://ffmpeg.org/download.html and https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git
           (release branch 8.1). Build scripts and the exact configuration:
           https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds

AirLens loads these libraries as separate DLLs at run time. You may replace
them with your own build of the same FFmpeg version.

FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.
